About APYIN

Who We Are

APYIN — the Association of Positive Youths Living with HIV/AIDS in Nigeria — is a youth-led network amplifying the voices of young people living with HIV across all 36 states.

Founded on the principles of community, solidarity, and human rights, APYIN works at the intersection of health, advocacy, and social justice. We train peer mentors, engage policymakers, provide psychosocial support, and partner with national and international organisations to champion a stigma-free Nigeria.

Our Osun State Chapter serves as a hub for youth advocacy in the Southwest, connecting members to networks like NEPWHAN, ASWHAN, GNP+, ICW, and ECEWS — ensuring every positive voice is heard.
